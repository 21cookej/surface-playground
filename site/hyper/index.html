4D objects

YW and ZW rotate the object into the extra dimension. Try 90° to turn a vertical or depth direction fully into W and change the 4D slice.

View & movement
Outside perspective

The observer follows the main camera in X/Z/W and keeps its Y slice aligned by default. At 0°: horizontal X/Z, vertical W; Y is held fixed. Rotate to mix X with W. Hidden parts are outside this slice.

How to explore

WASD move · Space / C rise and descend · drag to look · arrows look · V swap views · R reset.

In the outside view, drag to orbit. Red marks your position where it intersects the slice.

All three movement directions stay tangent to the curved space. You enter connected shapes continuously, without stepping through a flat portal.

Numerical prototype: finite ray distance and integration steps. Very tight curves and disconnected shapes have limits. The torus can contain different curvature signs in different directions.
